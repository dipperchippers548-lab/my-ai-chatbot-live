# LOADED IN — Pilot: "Day One"
Target length: ~12 minutes. One voice actor (you) plays everyone.

**How to read this:** `[NAME]` = who's talking. *(italics)* = how to say it. Lines in **ACTION** are what the camera sees (no need to record those).
Record each character in its own file if you can (e.g. `tee_scene3.mp3`), with a 1-second pause between lines — I'll cut them apart and place them.

## CAST
| Character | Who | Voice tip |
|---|---|---|
| **LAMAR** | You. Day one. Panics at everything, wants OUT. | Your natural voice, lots of energy. |
| **TEE** | Ringmaster/host of the whole world. Flies. Old CRT-TV head, his face is drawn on the screen. Always "on". Secretly the villain, but not as evil as he seems. | Big showman voice, too cheerful, fast. (I'll add a TV-speaker filter.) |

## SCENE 4 — THE RESIDENTS (3:30–6:00)
**ACTION:** Tee drags Lamar into the "Hub" — a colorful low-poly plaza. Jay is lying on a bench, tossing a cube in the air.
`[TEE]` Everyone! Say hello to Lamar!
`[JAY]` *(not looking)* Hello to Lamar.
`[LAMAR]` ...Is that a mouse?
`[JAY]` *(sits up, offended)* Is that a mouse? Is that a— buddy, you're a guy made of triangles. You don't get to ask questions.
`[TEE]` Jay's our comedian!
`[JAY]` I'm your PRISONER, Tee.
`[TEE]` *(laughing way too loud)* HAHA! Comedian!
**ACTION:** Nova bounces in, waving with both arms. Rhea walks behind her, unimpressed.
`[NOVA]` *(gasps)* A NEW ONE! Oh my gosh, hi! I'm Nova! Welcome! You're gonna LOVE it here!
`[RHEA]` *(flat)* He's not gonna love it here.
`[NOVA]` He's gonna love it here!
`[RHEA]` I'm Rhea. Don't touch the water. It's not water.
`[LAMAR]` What is it?
`[RHEA]` *(shrugs)* Nobody knows. Steve touched it.
`[LAMAR]` Who's Steve?
`[RHEA]` *(pause)* Exactly.
**ACTION:** Nova grabs Lamar's arm and gives a speed-tour, pointing at things.
`[NOVA]` *(fast, excited)* Okay so that's the Hub, that's the fountain, that's the tower, that's the other tower, that's the sky — it's purple on Tuesdays —
`[LAMAR]` How long have you guys been here?
**ACTION:** Everyone goes quiet. Beat.
`[NOVA]` *(smile frozen)* ...Six years! Feels like a weekend!
`[RHEA]` Six years. Feels like six years.
`[JAY]` *(tossing his cube)* Twelve. But who's counting. *(beat)* I'm counting.
`[LAMAR]` *(panicking)* TWELVE YEARS?!
**ACTION:** Rhea's arm glitches — loads in backwards for a second, then snaps back. She doesn't even react.
`[LAMAR]` WHAT WAS THAT?!
`[RHEA]` *(deadpan)* Tuesday.

## SCENE 5 — THE RULES (6:00–7:30)
**ACTION:** Tee pops up behind them holding a giant glowing scroll.
`[TEE]` Rule time! Rule one: every day there's a game!
`[TEE]` Rule two: everybody plays the game!
`[TEE]` Rule three: we don't talk about rule four!
`[LAMAR]` What's rule four?
`[TEE]` *(cheerful, instant)* We don't talk about it!
`[LAMAR]` Okay, okay — just tell me how to get out. Where's the exit?

`[LAMAR]` He didn't answer.
`[RHEA]` *(quietly)* He never does.

## SCENE 6 — THE ESCAPE (7:30–10:00)
**ACTION:** Lamar sprints away from the Hub toward the horizon. Jay strolls after him, eating cheese.
`[LAMAR]` *(running, out of breath)* There's gotta be an edge. Everything has an edge!
`[JAY]` *(calling after him)* Oh, he's running! Look at him go! Somebody get the popcorn!
**ACTION:** Lamar runs over a hill... and the Hub is right in front of him again. He stops.
`[LAMAR]` What?
**ACTION:** He turns around and runs the other way. Over a hill. The Hub again. Jay is sitting on the fountain waving.
`[JAY]` Welcome back! Did ya miss us?
`[LAMAR]` *(running again, yelling)* NO NO NO NO NO —
**ACTION:** Quick montage: Lamar runs left, right, digs a hole (pops out of the sky above the Hub), climbs a tower (the top is the Hub floor). Each time he lands back at the fountain. Jay is laughing harder each time.
`[JAY]` *(dying laughing)* HAHAHA the hole! He tried the HOLE!
`[LAMAR]` *(collapses, furious)* Stop LAUGHING! This isn't funny!
`[JAY]` It's a LITTLE funny!
`[LAMAR]` You've been here twelve years and you're just— what, you just gave up?! You didn't even TRY?!
**ACTION:** Jay stops laughing. The cheese drops. He walks up to Lamar, towering over him. Music cuts out.
`[JAY]` *(quiet, then building to a yell)* You think we didn't try that? You think I didn't run? I ran for THREE YEARS, man. I dug, I climbed, I jumped off every single thing in this place. *(yelling)* YOU THINK WE DIDN'T TRY THAT?!
**ACTION:** Silence. Jay's ear twitches. He turns away.
`[JAY]` *(back to joking, but it's forced)* ...Welcome to the show, rookie.
**ACTION:** Jay walks off. Lamar sits alone on the ground.

## SCENE 7 — BLANE (10:00–11:30)
**ACTION:** Night. The sky is a flat dark purple with square stars. Lamar sits on the edge of the fountain. Footsteps. Blane sits down next to him slowly, like an old man — even though he looks young.
`[BLANE]` *(calm, quiet)* First day's the worst.
`[LAMAR]` Who are you?
`[BLANE]` Blane. I don't come to the games much.
`[LAMAR]` How long have you been here? Let me guess. Twenty years?
`[BLANE]` *(small laugh)* Ninety.
`[LAMAR]` *(stares)* ...You don't look ninety.
`[BLANE]` Nobody ages here. *(beat)* That's not a gift, kid.
`[LAMAR]` How did you even— people didn't have VR ninety years ago.
`[BLANE]` *(long pause)* No. They didn't.
**ACTION:** Blane looks up at Tee's tower, where a single screen glows in the window.
`[BLANE]` *(serious)* Listen to me. Smile at the games. Play along. Don't ask Tee about the exit twice. And whatever you do... don't ever let yourself get used to it.
`[LAMAR]` Why not?
`[BLANE]` Because that's how he keeps you.
**ACTION:** Blane stands up and starts to walk away.
`[LAMAR]` Wait — how old were you? When you got here?
**ACTION:** Blane stops. Doesn't turn around.
`[BLANE]` *(quiet)* I was seven.
**ACTION:** He walks off into the dark. Lamar sits alone.

## SCENE 8 — CLIFFHANGER (11:30–12:00)
**ACTION:** Inside Tee's tower. Tee floats alone in front of a wall of tiny screens — each showing one of the residents sleeping. One screen shows Lamar, awake on the fountain.
`[TEE]` *(soft, almost sad, no showman voice)* ...Day one.
**ACTION:** Tee's screen-face flickers. For two frames, his face is not a smile — something tired and scared. Then it snaps back to the big grin.
`[TEE]` *(back to full showman, to nobody)* Big game tomorrow, folks! Don't touch that dial!
**ACTION:** Static. Cut to black. "LOADED IN" logo. Credits.
