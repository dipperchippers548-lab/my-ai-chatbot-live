He was working in a fire lookout tower when lightning hit it, and knocked off his big toenail. That was strike number one. Twenty seven years later, he was driving his truck, when lightning burned his eyebrows off. The next year, it hit his shoulder, in his own front yard. Then it struck him inside a ranger station, and set his hair on fire. A year later, his hair caught fire again. Then it hit his ankle. And the seventh time, he was out fishing. And as he walked away, a bear tried to steal his fish. He was struck by lightning seven times, and survived every single one.
